Why can't I add new bestsellers?

Books you borrow from Libby/OverDrive, buy on Kindle or Apple Books, or borrow from Open Library come locked with DRM. Only the store's own app can open them, so Kavita can't, and there's no API that hands out the files.

  • Buy DRM-free and upload it here: many publishers (Tor, Baen, Verso and others), Humble Bundle, Smashwords, itch.io, and Kobo books whose page says "DRM-free".
  • Reading a Libby book together anyway? You can each read it in Libby. The club tracks Kavita books only, so for that book progress and notes would have to be by hand.
